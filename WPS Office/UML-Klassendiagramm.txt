Beziehung zwischen Abschnitten

Hier würde ich keine direkte bidirektionale Komposition für previous/next speichern.

Stattdessen:

Document
│
│ 1
│
└── sections : List<Section>
       │
       ├── [0] Section
       │
       ├── [1] Section
       │
       ├── [2] Section
       │
       └── [3] Section

Die Navigation ergibt sich aus dem Index:

Section[0]
   │
   └── next() ──► Section[1]
                       │
                       └── next() ──► Section[2]
                                          │
                                          └── next() ──► Section[3]

Damit vermeidest du inkonsistente Zustände wie:

Section 2.next = Section 4
Section 4.previous = Section 3
Vollständiges Beziehungsmodell
Document
  ◆── 1..* Section

Section
  ◆── 1 DocumentContent
  ◆── 1 PageSetup
  ◆── 1 Header
  ◆── 1 Footer
  ◆── 1 PageNumbering
  ◇── 0..1 SectionBreak

DocumentContent
  ◆── 0..* Block

Block <<abstract>>
  △── Paragraph
  △── Table
  △── Image
  △── Shape
  △── PageBreak

Paragraph
  ◆── 0..* TextRun
  ─── 1 ParagraphProperties

Table
  ◆── 1..* Row

Row
  ◆── 1..* Cell

Cell
  ◆── 1 DocumentContent

PageSetup
  ◆── 1 Margins
  ◆── 1 PageSize
  ◆── 1 Columns
  ─── 1 Orientation

Header
  ◆── 1 DocumentContent

Footer
  ◆── 1 DocumentContent

PageNumbering
  ─── 1 PageNumberFormat
